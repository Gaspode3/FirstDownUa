ABOUT PROJECT

"First Down" is a project born out of friendship and respect.

Once, a close friend told Serhii Katrych, one of the first-responder bikers at NGO "Motodopomoga", that his childhood dream of riding a motorcycle had probably been shattered to pieces — after being wounded in the war (which led to the amputation of his left upper limb), nobody would ever let him behind the handlebars. Serhii decided to look into the situation and try to help Viking. And indeed, the motorcycle schools the responder approached refused to take on a veteran with one arm.

So Serhii bought an old, gentle Yamaha with his own money and found a mechanic who agreed to adapt it. In the end, he took on his friend's training himself. In time, an instructor from the Lemberg Moto school, Illia Tereshchenko, joined the process.

And when Viking not only mastered riding a motorcycle but also entered a moto gymkhana competition, it became clear that a project like this deserved to be continued and developed.

PROJECT MISSION

This project was created first and foremost for veterans, men and women wounded in combat, who have never ridden a motorcycle but have always dreamed of doing so. We will do everything we can so that you get to try it, and if you like it, learn to ride.

Around "First Down" we are also building a truly interesting and, most importantly, caring community, ready to develop the veteran "moto movement" and, step by step, build a system in which a veteran can officially obtain a Category "A" license even after being wounded.

WE PROVIDE A SAFE SPACE ON A CLOSED TRACK, ADAPTED MOTORCYCLES, QUALIFIED INSTRUCTORS, AND COMMUNITY SUPPORT.
